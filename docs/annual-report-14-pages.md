# Fourteen-page annual report

The data overview remains the entry screen. The modal report now has fourteen chapters, each using the selected report scope. No example counts are embedded in the presentation.

| Page | Chapter | Report values and visual form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Curiosity leaves a trace | Total messages, first/last activity; oversized number and orbit |
| 02 | One question opens a world | User messages, conversations, characters and assistant replies |
| 03 | Inspiration keeps its own time | Busiest hour, weekday and date in the selected timezone |
| 04 | Every week has your rhythm | Seven weekday message counts; alternating rounded columns |
| 05 | You kept showing up | Longest active streak, active days and a ten-week calendar ending on the last active date |
| 06 | A year with its own horizons | Busiest month across the selected scope and the latest twelve report months; skyline chart |
| 07 | A little further, together | Most-used model and model/attachment counts |
| 08 | Some conversations go further | Three longest conversations; numbered tickets with local titles |
| 09 | A few words. Or a whole world | Rounded mean user-message character length, longest user message and seven length buckets; horizontal ladder |
| 10 | Ideas come in many forms | Deduplicated image/audio/file counts; illustrated file cards |
| 11 | Your words. Your coordinates | User keywords; typography with staggered entrance |
| 12 | Some ideas are worth keeping | Starred, archived and shared conversation counts; a keepsake card |
| 13 | Your feedback has an echo | Recorded feedback and rating counts; a paper receipt |
| 14 | Keep a little of this year | Entry to the existing private share-card controls |

Paper, lavender, lime and ink establish the chapter rhythm. Added chapters use their own charts and objects rather than repeating the orbit illustration. Chart entrances respect reduced-motion preferences. Counts in charts retain full accessible labels; charts can receive keyboard focus.

Navigation still supports chapter buttons, previous/next buttons, arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End and horizontal touch gestures. Every transition resets the scroll position and focuses the heading. Escape closes the modal and returns focus. Short screens retain vertical scrolling.

Empty reports display zero counts or an unavailable marker without inventing activity. Older summaries without the full ranking use their saved longest-conversation list. Month charts are bounded to twelve entries, conversation tickets to three, and feedback rows to four plus an aggregated remainder. Archive, star and share states are counted independently and can overlap.

The report pages show no message excerpts or attachment filenames. Conversation titles and keywords remain local; the existing share-card opt-ins still control their inclusion in exports. Full statistics, parse warnings and JSON remain available from the modal toolbar.

Validation covers export-driven chapter values, empty data, old summaries, long titles, custom feedback labels, all chapter transitions, narrow screens, reduced motion and the existing import/filter/share flows. Screenshots also use the previously generated 510,000-message synthetic export's selected 2025 report.
